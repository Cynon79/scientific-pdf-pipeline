# Open-literature demonstration sources

Only attributed metadata and short, newly written paraphrases are included in `open_literature.json`. Full papers, figures, tables, and publisher Markdown are not bundled. Automated fixtures elsewhere are synthetic unless explicitly labeled otherwise.

- Luo, L. et al. (2018). *Heterogeneous catalytic ozonation of ciprofloxacin in aqueous solution using a manganese-modified silicate ore*. RSC Advances 8, 33534–33541. [Publisher and rights statement](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2018/ra/c8ra06880a), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Suitable as an aqueous-ozonation PDF input downloaded separately from the publisher.
- Ma, D. et al. (2023). *Catalytic ozonation mechanism over M1-N3C1 active sites*. Nature Communications 14, 7011. [Publisher and rights statement](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-42853-8), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Gas-phase counterexample: do not treat it as an aqueous validation result.

Rights statements were checked on publisher pages on 2026-10-11. The paraphrases simplify the authors' descriptions and are not quotations. Any separately downloaded material retains its source attribution and license; check individual third-party credit lines before reusing images. No HIT-access-only literature is used in this example set.

These two records are demonstration inputs, not a scientific benchmark and not the full sources used in the historical agent execution. The historical trace in the learning guide is a structural summary only.
